SUMMARY OF WORK COMPLETED:

1. PUBLIC REGISTRATION ENDPOINT (POST /auth/register)
   - Created public `POST /auth/register` endpoint in `AuthController` & `AuthService`.
   - Delegates user + profile creation to `UsersService.create()` in a Prisma `$transaction`.
   - Allows users to register directly as `STUDENT`, `TEACHER`, or `HOD` with their department and role-specific profile attributes.
   - Automatically signs and issues JWT access token and refresh cookie upon successful registration.

2. PROFILE MANAGEMENT ENDPOINT (PATCH /auth/profile)
   - Created protected `PATCH /auth/profile` endpoint in `AuthController`, `AuthService`, and `UsersService`.
   - Allows authenticated users to update personal details (firstName, lastName, phone, gender, dateOfBirth, profileImageUrl) and editable role profile fields (e.g. `section` for Student, `designation` & `qualification` for Teacher).
